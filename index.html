My process

How I work

TL;DR

I validate direction before I build, turn research into shared frameworks, build in regular rituals with users and the team, and document the patterns that outlast any one screen.

I validate direction before I build.

Painted-door pages, smoke tests, and structured feature surveys check real intent and prioritize what's worth the team pursuing.

I turn raw research into shared frameworks.

Interviews and diary studies don't move a team on their own, so I convert them into mental models that PM, engineering, and research can all point back to.

I build in regular rituals.

Design sprints get cross-functional teams to a decision, monthly lunch-and-learns keep the team in the room with real users, and open "UX roast" critiques keep the work honest between milestones.

I document the pattern.

For anything complex or multi-surface, I pull out the reusable components and feed them into a shared design system so the next surface doesn't start from zero.

AI in My Practice

How I use AI in design work

I use AI where it speeds up the parts of design that usually lose momentum, while staying genuinely curious rather than performatively fluent:

  • Research synthesis: I cluster patterns across interviews and usability findings to shorten the path from data to insight.
  • Early concepting: I generate rough visual directions and interaction patterns to fuel critique early, before anything is finished.
  • Prototyping: I move through more iterations in the same window, comparing real options instead of one polished direction.
  • Communication: I draft copy, docs, and presentations faster, leaving more time for the argument and less for the scaffolding.
